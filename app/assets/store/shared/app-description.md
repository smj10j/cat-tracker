# Whisker Health — App Description (Canonical)

This is the canonical app description. Store-specific files (iOS description.txt, Android description.txt) derive from this source.

## One-liner
Evidence-based cat health tracking with veterinary-grade insights.

## Tagline
Track your cat's health with the care they deserve.

## Full Description
Whisker Health logs weight, food intake, hydration, grooming, activity, and litter habits — then connects the dots with correlation insights backed by veterinary guidelines (AAFP, WSAVA, ISFM).

### Key Features
- **Weight tracking** with trend charts and clinically-sourced health alerts
- **Daily check-in** for quick multi-measurement logging
- **Medication & care schedule** with push notification reminders
- **Correlation engine** that spots patterns (e.g., food drop → weight loss) weeks before they become emergencies
- **Vet export**: print-ready PDF with weight history, behavioral trends, and clinical observations
- **Household sharing** so everyone caring for your cat stays in sync
- **In Memoriam**: gentle way to honor cats who have passed away while preserving their health records

### Trust & Privacy
Every health threshold cites its source — AAFP, WSAVA, ISFM, or peer-reviewed feline medicine journals. No thresholds are invented or AI-generated.

Free. No ads. No tracking. No data sales. Your cats' data stays yours.
