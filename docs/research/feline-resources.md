# Feline Reference Directory

Curated list of authoritative sources for clinical content in Cat Tracker. Consult these when adding new measurement types, modifying thresholds, or writing wellness copy.

---

## Clinical guideline bodies (Tier 1)

### AAFP — American Association of Feline Practitioners
- **URL:** aafponline.org
- **Relevance:** Feline-specific clinical practice guidelines; the primary US body for feline medicine standards. Publishes consensus guidelines on nutrition, pain management, hyperthyroidism, senior care, FLUTD, and more.
- **Key guidelines used in Cat Tracker:** Nutritional Guidelines, Pain Management Guidelines, Senior Care Guidelines, FLUTD/FIC Consensus Statement, Hyperthyroidism Management Guidelines

### WSAVA — World Small Animal Veterinary Association
- **URL:** wsava.org (Global Nutrition Toolkit available for download)
- **Relevance:** International standards for nutritional assessment, life-stage care, and vaccination. WSAVA publishes the Nutritional Assessment Guidelines used for weight-change rate thresholds in Cat Tracker.
- **Key guidelines used:** Nutritional Assessment Guidelines, Life Stage Guidelines

### ISFM — International Society of Feline Medicine
- **URL:** icatcare.org/isfm
- **Relevance:** ISFM is the feline medicine arm of International Cat Care (iCatCare). Publishes consensus guidelines on feline stress, environmental enrichment, musculoskeletal pain, and welfare.
- **Key guidelines used:** Feline Stress Guidelines, Musculoskeletal Pain Index, Feline Environmental Needs Guidelines

### IRIS — International Renal Interest Society
- **URL:** iris-kidney.com
- **Relevance:** Global authority on CKD staging and management in cats and dogs. CKD is one of the most common diagnoses in cats over age 7; IRIS staging is used worldwide.

### ACVIM — American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine
- **URL:** acvim.org
- **Relevance:** Publishes consensus statements on cardiac disease (HCM), seizures, and other internal medicine topics; referenced for emergency signs in Cat Tracker.

## Peer-reviewed journals (Tier 1)

### Journal of Feline Medicine and Surgery (JFMS)
- Publisher: SAGE / ISFM
- Primary feline-specific journal; AAFP guidelines are published here

### Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine (JVIM)
- Publisher: Wiley / ACVIM
- Publishes high-impact small animal internal medicine research; hepatic lipidosis studies referenced in Cat Tracker appear here

### Veterinary Clinics of North America: Small Animal Practice
- Publisher: Elsevier
- In-depth clinical reviews; weight loss thresholds and nutritional management are covered in dedicated issues

### Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association (JAVMA)
- Publisher: AVMA
- Broad-scope; nutrition and epidemiology studies referenced in Cat Tracker

## Consumer-facing trusted references (Tier 2)

### Cornell Feline Health Center
- **URL:** vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center
- **Use for:** Wellness Guide copy; owner-facing explanations of clinical concepts
- Affiliated with Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine; consumer content is reviewed by veterinary faculty

### Merck Veterinary Manual (Owner Section)
- **URL:** merckvetmanual.com → "Cat Owners" section
- **Use for:** Owner-facing description of conditions mentioned in alert copy
- Produced by Merck in partnership with veterinary authors; well-regarded as a reference for non-specialists

### iCatCare / International Cat Care
- **URL:** icatcare.org
- **Use for:** Behavioral content; stress and enrichment guidance
- The consumer arm of ISFM; content reviewed by feline medicine specialists

### ASPCA
- **URL:** aspca.org/pet-care/cat-care
- **Use for:** General wellness guidance; not appropriate for numeric thresholds
- Useful for poison control information (aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control)

## Reference textbooks

These are not freely available online but are standard references in feline medicine:

- **Ettinger & Feldman** — *Textbook of Veterinary Internal Medicine*, 8th ed. — comprehensive internal medicine reference; covers all body systems
- **Drobatz & Costello** — *Feline Emergency and Critical Care Medicine* — source for emergency sign severity; ATE, respiratory distress
- **Horwitz & Mills** — *BSAVA Manual of Canine and Feline Behavioural Medicine* — behavioral indicators

## When to use each tier

| Context | Source tier |
|---------|------------|
| Adding or changing a numeric threshold (rate, %, count) | Tier 1 only |
| Alert copy naming a specific condition (hepatic lipidosis, FLUTD) | Tier 1 only |
| Wellness Guide informational text | Tier 1 or Tier 2 |
| Correlation description clinical context | Tier 1 preferred; Tier 2 for consumer framing |
| Vet export Methodology section citations | Tier 1 only |
